# Builder self-check

This is the author's repair pass. The host commissions independent review through
[docs/orchestrator.md](../../../docs/orchestrator.md) and [docs/review.md](../../../docs/review.md).
Do not submit a self-check as independent approval or fabricate a review receipt.

Run the pipeline build and inspect diagnostics. Choose actual snapshot times for the opening,
important visual beats, rule/exhibit cards, scary reveals and ending. Include frames immediately
before and after seams. Inspect at the saved canvas and phone size. Use playback to judge motion.

## Questions that find defects

| Question | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Does the image read as the intended event? | Subject, props and action at the spoken anchor |
| Does contact hold? | Hands on handles, feet on floors, reach and limb continuity through the move |
| Can the viewer follow the space? | Door sides, eyelines, recurring props and cuts |
| Is the important thing legible? | Contrast, scale, glow, compression, caption and HUD occlusion |
| Does the series frame stay correct? | Identity, number, rule progress, captions and finish |
| Does timing support the first viewing? | Reveal, readable hold, anticipation and reaction at playback speed |
| Do repeated timestamps match? | Same frame before and after a backward seek |
| Is the final media actually correct? | Encoded frames and verified soundtrack, when produced by the host |

An intentional still hold, hard cut, black ground or HUD is not inherently a defect. Explain a concrete
viewer problem before proposing a change. Automated bounds or signal measurements cannot decide
whether the event is clear, frightening or smooth.

Record repair notes with a timestamp, location, observed problem, exact change and what must remain.
Prioritize broken meaning, unreadable text and technical failures before polish. Rebuild after source
changes and inspect affected moments and seams. Preserve approved narration and successful visuals;
fix a faulty limb or prop in its source rather than covering it with another copy.

Report what was measured, viewed and played separately. Audio levels do not establish that sound was
heard; a contact sheet does not establish motion quality. State unavailable inspection and unresolved
findings in the handoff. The pipeline owns frozen evidence, review reports and delivery qualification.
