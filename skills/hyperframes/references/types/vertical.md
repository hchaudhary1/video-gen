# Vertical horror Shorts

Use the saved job canvas and fps. A vertical version is recomposed for that frame, not squeezed from
landscape. The rules-story profile owns length, opening, identity continuity and looping ending;
this reference does not replace its pacing with generic social-video averages.

## Stage for a phone

- Keep the trigger and relevant props visible from frame one. The hook must read with captions
  and picture alone as well as with sound.
- Give the important subject enough width. Stack relationships vertically when a side-by-side layout
  makes both objects too small. Preserve geography needed to understand action and consequence.
- Reserve space for the kit's HUD, rule progress and sentence captions. Use its vertical layout
  rather than adding another caption track or restyling the series frame.
- Phone app controls cover edges and the right-hand action rail. The repo's current planning safe area
  (`lib/media.mjs`, `safeArea`) is x 6–87% and y 11–80% of the canvas. Treat that as a composition guide,
  not a guarantee for every platform. Inspect the target presentation when available.
- Keep text short and large enough to read at phone width. If it does not fit, recompose the picture
  or use existing caption-generation controls; do not rewrite approved narration to fit a box.

## Cuts and the loop

Use measured sentence and action anchors. A Short still needs setup, action and consequence; speeding
every move can erase the scare. Check the last-to-first seam in playback so the intended loop is clear,
while preserving the identity's required ending. Inspect cuts, caption changes and subject contact at
phone size. A landscape snapshot alone does not validate a vertical adaptation.
